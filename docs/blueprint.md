# **App Name**: Aromatic Flames

## Core Features:

- Candle Showcase: Display high-quality images of candles on the home page with a clean and attractive design.
- Contact Form: Implement a contact form that captures user details (name, phone, email, message) and sends an email to saatvik.shrivastava08@gmail.com.
- Product Catalog: Showcase available candles with images, prices, specifications, and filtering options (price, scent, color).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm beige or cream to evoke a sense of natural soy wax.
- Secondary color: Earthy brown tones to complement the natural theme.
- Accent: Gold (#FFD700) for highlights, calls to action, and a premium feel.
- Clean, minimalist layout with plenty of white space to highlight product images and descriptions.
- Use simple, elegant icons for navigation and filters.
- Subtle fade-in animations for images and text to create a smooth user experience.

## Original User Request:
Create a modern, elegant, and visually appealing website for a homemade scented candle business. The candles are handmade in India using eco wicks and 100% natural soy wax, with a burn time of up to 40 hours. The website should include:

Home Page – Display high-quality, aesthetic images of candles with a clean and attractive UI/UX design.

About Us – Share a brief story about the brand and the (fictional or placeholder) owner to add a personal touch.

Contact Us Page – Include a contact form that captures the user's name, phone number, email, and message. The form should send an email to saatvik.shrivastava08@gmail.com for follow-up.

Store Page – Showcase available candles with their images, prices, and specifications. Include filters (e.g., price range, scent type, wax color) to help users browse effectively.

Ensure the design feels handcrafted, warm, and premium, reflecting the artisanal nature of the product. Prioritize good UI/UX practices across all pages.
